ABOUT ME

I am a Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate at IIIT Sonipat, driven by a curiosity that extends beyond the classroom. I am interested in understanding not just how technology works, but why it works — from the logic behind algorithms to the mathematical foundations of computing and the scientific ideas that shape our world.
I believe in learning by building, experimenting, and questioning assumptions. Through programming, personal projects, and problem-solving, I am working to strengthen my fundamentals while exploring how theoretical concepts translate into practical systems.
I am particularly interested in the intersection of computer science, mathematics, and scientific research. I hope to contribute to projects where rigorous thinking, genuine curiosity, and a willingness to learn matter more than simply having all the answers.
I am always looking for opportunities to learn from people who think deeply, work on meaningful problems, and challenge me to think beyond what I already know.
